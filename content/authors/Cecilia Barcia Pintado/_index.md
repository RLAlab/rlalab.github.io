Hi, I am Cecilia Barcia Pintado. I recently graduated from the University of Edinburgh with a BSc in Biochemistry. During my degree, I was a member of the Edinburgh iDEC team, where I worked on plastic degradation. I also worked in the Wallace Lab for my dissertation project, focusing on the valorisation of bread waste using engineered _E. coli_ and exploring how fluorescence could be used to monitor bacterial growth. I am currently an MRes student in Alternative Proteinsand my project focuses on producing colourants using _Yarrowia lipolytica_. I am particularly interested in using bioengineering to develop microbial cell factories as alternatives to traditional production methods, and I’m excited to explore this through my project.  
Outside of the lab, I love hiking, tennis, and surfing. I am also a big foodie and always open to trying new things!
